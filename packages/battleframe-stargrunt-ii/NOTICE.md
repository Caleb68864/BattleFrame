# NOTICE — BattleFrame: Stargrunt II

This module implements the **mechanics** of *Stargrunt II*, a science-fiction
skirmish wargame by **Ground Zero Games**.

## What this module is

A rules **engine** for the BattleFrame system. It encodes how Stargrunt II's
mechanics work — the polyhedral die-shift ladder, the multiple-opposed firefight
resolution, suppression, the confidence/reaction morale tests, and the
two-action alternating activation order — and lets you enter your own unit,
weapon, and armour data.

## What this module is NOT

It ships **no ruleset content**:

- no rulebook text, prose, or flavour;
- no artwork;
- no proprietary unit stat blocks, weapon tables, armour tables, points values,
  or scenario text.

Game mechanics and numeric relationships are facts and are not the expression
that copyright protects; this module reproduces none of Stargrunt II's
copyrightable expression. To play, you supply your own unit and weapon values
(from a rulebook or army list you own).

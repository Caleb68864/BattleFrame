# Equipment

The **only** differentiator between knights ([[knights-have-no-stat-line]]). The QSR references equipment but never provides a list, costs, or restrictions.

## What the QSR confirms

Three equipment concepts appear, solely inside [[clash-bonuses]]:

| Equipment | Effect |
|---|---|
| **One-handed weapon** | +1 to **light melee** attacks (face 2) |
| **Two-handed weapon** | +1 to **heavy melee** attacks (face 1) |
| **Shield** | +2 to **Block** defense ([[clash-defenses]]) |

Plus QSR p1: "You may perform a light or heavy melee attack with **any weapon a knight is equipped with**."

And the armour table is titled "**heavy** armor table" ([[heavy-armor-table]]), implying armour types exist as a category.

## The interesting structural bit

Weapon bonuses are **bound to specific initiative steps**. A one-handed knight wants 2s; a two-handed knight wants 1s. So your loadout determines *which faces of your dice pool are valuable to you* — equipment reaches back and reshapes the [[initiative-dice-are-the-action-menu]] economy. Two warbands with different loadouts want different rolls. `confidence: unverified` — my analysis.

## Full-game equipment glimpsed (NOT in QSR)

From Goonhammer (https://www.tabletopbattles.com/goonhammer-reviews-greathelm-a-micro-skirmish-game-of-chivalric-fantasy/), describing advanced rules:

- **Light armour**: "will instead take that extra damage on a 1-3 while having +1 to all dodge rolls and also +1" to all moves" — i.e. armour type swaps the armour table *and* modifies movement.
- **Armour reducing Bash distance**: "a piece of armour that only make him move 1" when Bashed instead of the normal 3"."
- **Bows and crossbows**: "Bows shoot during initiative step 2 while crossbows are slower and can be shot during initiative step 1." — ranged weapons slot into the same step system.
- **Items**: resolve "during initiative step 4 after most movement has happened but before any attacks."

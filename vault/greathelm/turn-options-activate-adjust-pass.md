# Turn Options — Activate, Adjust, or Pass

On your turn you choose **exactly one** of three things. QSR p1, verbatim:

> on your turn, choose one:
> 1. **Knight activation:** Choose a knight to perform an action matching a dice in the current initiative step. Your turn ends.
> 2. **Dice adjust:** Rotate one of your highest initiative dice down one number. Your turn ends.
> 3. **Pass:** If you have no dice in the current initiative step, you must pass.

## Notes on each

1. **Knight activation** — the die's face must equal the current step. See [[dice-face-to-action-mapping]].
2. **Dice adjust** — see [[dice-adjust-rotate-down]]. Costs your whole turn.
3. **Pass** — note the phrasing: passing is **mandatory when you have no dice in the current step**, not an option you may elect. The QSR does not describe a voluntary pass while holding a valid die. `confidence: partial` — the rule states the compulsory case only; whether you *may* pass while holding a legal die is **not found**. Read strictly, "choose one" + the conditional on option 3 implies you may not.

Each option **ends your turn**, so play strictly alternates one decision at a time.

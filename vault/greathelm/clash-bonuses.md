# Clash Bonuses

Added to the d6 in a [[clash-test]]. QSR p2, verbatim ("these are added to the d6 roll"):

> +1 for each **outnumbering** ally.
> +1 for each **momentum** spent. (attacker only)
> +1 to **light melee** attacks for **one-handed** weapon.
> +1 to **heavy melee** attacks for **two-handed** weapon.
> +2 to **block** defense for **shield**.

## Reading the list

- **Outnumbering** — available to *both* sides. See [[outnumbering]].
- **Momentum** — attacker only, unbounded by the rule text ("any amount"), so max +3 given the cap of 3. See [[momentum]].
- **Weapon bonuses are action-matched**, and this is the interesting bit: a one-handed weapon helps *only* Light Melee (face 2); a two-handed weapon helps *only* Heavy Melee (face 1). Your equipment therefore determines **which initiative step you want dice in**. See [[equipment]].
- **Shield** applies only to the Block defense — not to Parry, Riposte, or Dodge.

## Magnitude

On a d6 opposed roll, +1 is large and +2 is decisive. A shielded Blocker (+2) with two outnumbering allies (+2) sits at +4 on a d6 — the attacker essentially cannot win without spending momentum. Positioning dominates dice. `confidence: unverified` — my analysis, not a source claim.

## Not found

No source states whether bonuses stack without limit, or whether a knight can hold both a one-handed weapon *and* a two-handed weapon and pick per attack. QSR p1 says only: "You may perform a light or heavy melee attack with any weapon a knight is equipped with."

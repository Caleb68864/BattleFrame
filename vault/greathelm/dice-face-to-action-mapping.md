# Dice Face → Action Mapping

A die's **face value hard-selects which action it may buy**. It is not a currency amount and not a to-hit number. This is the central table of the game.

QSR p1, verbatim (QSR names; the p5 reference-card names are in parentheses):

| Face | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **6** | Sprint (Run) | Move up to **5"** and gain **2 momentum** |
| **5** | Encircle (Walk) | Move up to **3"** and gain **1 momentum** |
| **4** | Bash | **Clash test!** Success moves the defender up to 3" and they lose all momentum |
| **3** | Shift (Step) | Move up to **1"** |
| **2** | Light Melee attack (Fast attack) | **Clash test!** Success deals **1 damage marker** |
| **1** | Heavy Melee attack (Heavy attack) | **Clash test!** Success deals **2 damage markers** |

> Actions marked Clash test! require the knight to be in base contact with an enemy, and trigger a dice roll-off (see Clash Tests)

## The core tension

High dice buy **mobility and momentum**; low dice buy **damage**. A great initiative roll (lots of 6s) wins you the *choice* of turn order and lots of movement — but leaves you unable to hurt anyone. The 6s that win [[initiative-order-determination]] are the same 6s that cannot attack. That inverse coupling is the design's cleverest joint.

## Steps 6/5/3 auto-succeed

Only 4, 2, 1 are [[clash-test]] actions. Goonhammer: "Initiative steps 4, 2, and 1 are all clash test actions which means they won't automatically happen, you have to roll some dice!" Movement never fails.

## Action names corroborated

Run / Walk / Bash / Step / Fast Attacks / Heavy Attacks — confirmed independently by the licensed initiative tray product: https://buythesametoken.com/products/initiative-tray-and-token-set-for-greathelm

## Distance discrepancy

QSR v0.4 says Sprint = **5"**. Goonhammer says Run = **6"**. See [[version-discrepancies-qsr-vs-kickstarter]] — treat 5" as current.

# Warband Construction — NOT FOUND

**No source found describes a warband construction system.** This note exists to record the gap, not to fill it.

## What is confirmed

- Each side has **6 knights** (QSR p1: "Repeat until each player has all 6 knights on the field"; back cover: "a micro-skirmish game of 6 vs. 6 knights").
- Knights are differentiated **only** by [[equipment]], which the QSR never enumerates.
- Knights have **no stat line** in the QSR ([[knights-have-no-stat-line]]).
- The game is **miniatures-agnostic** (itch.io: "a tabletop miniatures agnostic skirmish game").
- The marketing hook is "one sprue = one warband" — a standard 6-figure plastic sprue builds a full side. (Spruedude: https://spruedude.com/blogs/gaming/greathelm-finally-a-game-where-one-sprue-one-warband-huzzah — but this article explicitly defers mechanics: "Next time: Lets talk about how Greathelm plays!")

## NOT FOUND — do not invent

- **Points system** — no evidence found either way. Do not assume points exist.
- **Knight roles / types / classes** — none found.
- **Stat lines** — none in the QSR.
- **List-building constraints** — none found.
- **Whether the 6 is fixed** or a scenario variable.
- **Leader / champion rules** — none found. (The back cover's "focusing on the most important knights" hints at asymmetry, but this is marketing copy, not a rule.)

## Assessment

The QSR is explicitly "an introductory ruleset", so its silence is weak evidence. The full game clearly has *more* equipment ([[equipment]] — light armour, bows, crossbows, items), which implies **some** selection system. But whether that's points, a menu, or scenario-fixed loadouts is entirely unknown.

**Architectural guidance:** do not design a list-builder from this research. Model equipment as an open-ended list on the knight and defer the construction layer until the full rulebook is available. See [[open-questions]].

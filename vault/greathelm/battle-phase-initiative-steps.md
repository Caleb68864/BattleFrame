# Battle Phase — Descending Initiative Steps

The battle phase walks **down** the die faces, 6 → 1. The current face value is the "initiative step".

QSR p1, verbatim:

> Starting with whoever goes first, players alternate, one activation at a time, working through the current initiative step before moving down to the next.

Kickstarter states the step-advance rule more precisely:

> Initiative dice are spent in a strict order. The highest value die in play is the current initiative step. When all dice of the current initiative step are spent, the next highest die value in play immediately becomes the new initiative step.

— https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1674560143/greathelm

## Key properties

- The step is **global**, derived from the highest unspent die *in play* (across both players) — so empty steps are skipped entirely.
- Alternation is **one action at a time**, not one model at a time.
- The phase ends when all dice are spent, which triggers [[courage-phase]].

## Why the descending order is the game

The [[dice-face-to-action-mapping]] means the round has a fixed dramatic arc: movement resolves first (6/5), then Bash (4), then a last repositioning chance (3), then attacks (2/1). You cannot attack before your opponent has finished moving. Positioning is therefore always committed *before* violence, every round.

The Kickstarter describes the arc: step 3 is "the last chance your knights have to make an adjustment, or step away from harms way, because now the turn is going to turn deadly!"

# Victory Condition (Quickstart) — Last Man Standing

QSR p2, verbatim:

> **round and game ends**
> If only one player has knights remaining in the play area, they win! If not, start a new round from the initiative phase.

## Scope caveat — important

This is **the quickstart's scenario only**, not the game's scoring system. QSR p1 says so explicitly:

> These quick start rules contain an introductory ruleset and a **simple scenario objective: Last man standing!**

So: **no VP, no objectives, no round limit in the QSR.** Do not conclude the full game lacks them — it evidently has scenarios. Goonhammer: "Each scenario will tell you how many rounds to play." And [[scenes]] describes scenarios chaining multiple scenes on victory.

**The full game's objective and scoring system is `not found`.** Do not design scoring from this note.

## Mechanics of the check

- Checked after the [[courage-phase]], i.e. once per round.
- Knights leave "the play area" by **damage** (3 markers) or by **fleeing** a failed [[courage-test]] — both count identically.
- Phrased as "only one player has knights remaining" — a mutual wipe-out is therefore **not** a win for either side, and is not addressed. Draw handling is **not found**.

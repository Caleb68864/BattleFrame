# Initiative Phase

First phase of each round. Three steps, QSR p1 verbatim:

> After gathering your dice, roll them and organize them into rows of matching numbers in order from highest to lowest.
>
> You may re-roll any dice that are not 6's, once.

1. **Gather** — see [[initiative-dice-pool-size]].
2. **Roll** all of them.
3. **Organize** into rows of matching faces, descending. This is not decoration — the rows *are* the [[battle-phase-initiative-steps]] the round will walk down. A physical initiative tray is sold for exactly this.
4. **Re-roll** — optional, once, any dice that are not 6s. You may re-roll a subset (the rule says "any dice"), so it is a per-die choice, not all-or-nothing.

## Re-roll subtleties

- 6s can never be re-rolled — you keep them.
- The re-roll is a gamble: a 5 (Walk) re-rolled hoping for a 6 (Run) can become a 1 (Heavy attack). Since **every face is a usable action**, there is no "bad" result, only a differently-shaped round. This makes the re-roll a genuinely interesting decision rather than a strict upgrade. `confidence: unverified` — my reading, not stated in any source.
- Whether re-rolled dice can be re-rolled again: no — "once."

Goonhammer mentions a re-roll ("You can also re-roll some dice, too") without detailing the trigger; the QSR resolves it as above.

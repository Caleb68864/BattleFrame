# Play Area and Setup

## Play area

A single sheet of paper. QSR p1 components: "A standard paper sized battlefield (letter/A4)". Kickstarter: "Play Area: 8.5"x11" battlefield", noting the standard is "US letter 8.5"x 11" or A4 8.3"x 11.7"," with "an alternative standard for games is a 12"x 12"."

The play area is called a **scene** — see [[scenes]].

Goonhammer notes it scales: "it could absolutely work with 15mm miniatures if you wanted to halve your distances."

## Deployment

QSR p1, verbatim:

> Each player rolls a d6. Starting with whoever rolled highest, alternate placing one knight anywhere on the battlefield, at least 1" away from any edge. Knights may be placed in base contact with enemies. Repeat until each player has all 6 knights on the field.

Properties:
- **Free placement** — anywhere, no deployment zones. Deployment is not "your half"; it's an interleaved draft across the whole board.
- Only constraint: **≥1" from any edge**.
- **You may deploy into base contact with the enemy** — combat can begin on round 1 with no approach.
- Alternating placement means the last placer has full information — a real first-turn asymmetry that the d6 roll-off decides.

Note this deployment roll-off is a **separate d6**, distinct from [[initiative-order-determination]] (which counts 6s in the pool).

## Components (QSR p1)

- 6 knights and **8 six-sided dice** (per player) — 8, not the 7 you start with, presumably slack for the [[initiative-phase]] re-roll.
- A device for measuring distance in inches.
- Tokens for damage and momentum.
- Letter/A4 battlefield.
- Optional initiative tracker printout.
- Free papercraft knights + battlefield included (QSR pp3–4).

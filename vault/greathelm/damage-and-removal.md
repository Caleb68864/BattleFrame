# Damage and Removal

Tracked with **damage markers**. Cap **3**. QSR p2, verbatim:

> **damage limit**
> All Knights have a damage limit of 3.
> When knights gain 3 or more damage markers, they are immediately removed from play.

## Damage sources

| Source | Damage |
|---|---|
| Light Melee attack (face 2), on success | 1 |
| Heavy Melee attack (face 1), on success | 2 |
| Riposte defense, on success | 1 to the attacker, **no armor roll** |
| Pierced result on [[heavy-armor-table]] | declared damage **+1** |
| Bash | **none** |

## Removal is immediate

"immediately removed from play" — not at end of phase. This matters: removal instantly shrinks that player's [[initiative-dice-pool-size]] for *subsequent rounds*, and instantly removes an [[outnumbering]] contributor mid-round.

## Two routes off the table

A knight leaves play by **damage** (3 markers) *or* by fleeing a failed [[courage-test]]. Both count for [[victory-condition-quickstart]], which only asks who "has knights remaining in the play area".

Note the coupling: accumulated damage *also* makes the courage test harder ([[courage-test]] difficulty is +1 per damage on the knight). So a knight on 2 damage is doubly fragile — one hit from removal, and testing at +2 difficulty. Damage is not just a hit-point counter; it's a morale multiplier.

## Scale

Three markers means a Heavy attack (2) kills in two landed hits. Games are short and lethal. There is no healing mechanic in the QSR — **not found**.

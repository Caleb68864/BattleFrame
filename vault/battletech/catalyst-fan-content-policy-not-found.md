# No Catalyst Fan-Content Licence Exists (searched; NOT FOUND)

The task asked me to "find and quote Catalyst's actual fan-use/licensing policy if one exists."

**It does not exist, as far as I can establish.** Reporting this as a negative finding rather than guessing.

## What I searched
- `battletech.com/legal/` and `bg.battletech.com/legal/` — **`bg.battletech.com` no longer resolves** (DNS failure); `battletech.com` is a placeholder ([[battletech-com-site-rebuild-2026]])
- Catalyst's forum thread *"Boundaries for Creating Fan-based Tools"* (topic 14024)
- General web search for a Catalyst fan-content/fan-use policy

## What the "Boundaries for Creating Fan-based Tools" thread actually is

Critically — and contrary to what its title suggests — **this thread contains no official policy.** It is a *forum user* (`bronzite`, not a Catalyst representative) **asking** what fan tools may include. He proposed a heuristic:

> "Is it now or was it quite recently available on the Battletech.com website?"

**No Catalyst representative answered with binding boundaries in the fetched content.** A fan's proposed heuristic is not a licence and must not be relied on.

## What Catalyst's policy language actually says (opposite direction)

The only Catalyst policy language located runs *against* fan submissions:

> Catalyst Game Labs's long-standing policy does not allow it to accept or consider creative ideas, suggestions, or materials other than those it has specifically requested.

And their ToS takes a broad grant *from* users:

> By uploading materials to any forum… users automatically grant… a perpetual, royalty-free, irrevocable, non-exclusive right and license to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, publish, translate, create derivative works from and distribute such materials.

## Evidence Catalyst actively polices this

In 2022 Catalyst published a *Statement Regarding Online BattleTech Communities*, and reporting indicates Catalyst was **contacted by the BattleTech license holder** (i.e. Topps) regarding potential licence violations, after which Catalyst proactively contacted community members about IP use.

**Conclusion:** there is no permissive fan licence to rely on, and the licensor has demonstrably leaned on Catalyst about community IP use. Assume **no permission** by default. See [[licensing-verdict-battletech]].

# Catalyst Officially Publishes ~49 Free BattleTech PDFs

Catalyst maintains a `game-downloads` page whose own framing text reads:

> **The following free downloadable PDF products** will launch you into the [BattleTech universe]…
> These PDFs have everything you need to dive into the various ways in which you can enjoy the [game].

I counted **49 distinct free PDFs** linked from that page. Confirmed categories:

## Rules
- `Alpha Strike Quick Start Rules 2019-08.pdf` — see [[alpha-strike-quick-start-is-free]]
- `BattleForce_QSR.pdf`
- `Beginner-Box-Quick-Start-Rules.pdf`
- `QSR-Rulebook-ESSENTIALS.pdf`
- `ATOW QSR.pdf` (A Time of War RPG)
- `CAT35600_ChaosCampaign_Rulebook.pdf`
- `BatttleTech-Mercenaries-Rulebook-1st-Print.pdf`
- `CBTMiniRules_Final.pdf`

## Record sheets (pre-filled unit stats — the interesting part)
- `BattleTech-Record-Sheets-Alpha-Strike-Box-Set.pdf` ← **free Alpha Strike cards**
- `RS AGoAC Epub 1-26-19a.pdf` (A Game of Armored Combat)
- ~20 × `ForcePack-Record-Sheets-*.pdf` (Kell Hounds, Gray Death Legion, UrbanMechs, House Davion, Wolf's Dragoons, …)
- `E-CAT35001RS BattleTech Blank Record Sheets.pdf`
- `Record-Sheets-Mercenaries-Box-Set.pdf`
- Long Tom artillery errata sheets

## Counters / maps
- `BattleForce_Counters.pdf`, `BattleForce_Tokens_Mercs.pdf`
- `ISAW_HexMap_IS_2830.pdf`, `BattleTech-MapPack-Alien-Worlds-RULES.pdf`

## Critical caveat — "free" ≠ "licensed for reuse"

Catalyst calls these **free downloadable products**. That is a statement about *price*, not about *redistribution rights*. Not one of these PDFs carries an open licence, an SRD grant, or a fan-use permission. Free-to-download is not free-to-ship.

See [[free-to-read-vs-free-to-ship]] — this distinction is the crux of the whole licensing question.

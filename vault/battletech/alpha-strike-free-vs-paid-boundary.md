# Exactly What's Free vs Paid in Alpha Strike

The task asked to be **precise** here, because it decides whether a module can ship content.

## FREE (confirmed — [[battletech-official-free-downloads]])

| Item | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Alpha Strike Quick Start Rules (2019 Commander's Ed. Update)** | 39pp: basics, setup, movement/combat/end phases, **some** special abilities, 3 scenarios, reference tables, printable cards |
| `BattleTech-Record-Sheets-Alpha-Strike-Box-Set.pdf` | **Pre-filled Alpha Strike cards** for the box-set units |
| `BattleForce_QSR.pdf` + counters/tokens | BattleForce (AS's ancestor) |
| ~20 × ForcePack record sheets | Pre-filled unit stats |
| `CAT35600_ChaosCampaign_Rulebook.pdf` | Campaign framework |

**Also effectively free:** every unit's Alpha Strike stat line, via the [[master-unit-list-api]] — arguably the most significant "free" AS content of all, and it covers *thousands* of units, not just a starter set.

## PAID

| Item | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Alpha Strike: Commander's Edition** | The **complete** ruleset — the QSR is explicitly a subset |
| *Alpha Strike Box Set* | Minis + cards |
| Technical Readouts / Record Sheets (Unabridged) | Full unit catalogues |

Catalyst's own framing of free record sheets confirms the upsell boundary:

> Beyond these free record sheets, players will find the full line of print record sheets… as well as PDF-only **Unabridged** record sheets with all the various units and variants.

## What the QSR omits (partial — inferred from structure)

The QSR is a **teaser**, so expect it to lack: the full Special Abilities glossary, advanced/optional rules, aerospace, ground-vehicle/infantry/battle-armor detail, construction & PV computation, and full campaign rules. **I did not verify the exact omissions** — the live QSR PDF is currently down ([[battletech-com-site-rebuild-2026]]) and I did not access paid material. **Marking partial.**

## The decisive point

**Free vs paid is the wrong axis for the shipping decision.** Even the 100%-free tier carries **no reuse licence** ([[free-to-read-vs-free-to-ship]]). A module can't ship the free QSR's text any more than it can ship Commander's Edition's.

What "free" *actually* buys the project:
1. **Legitimate research access** — the mechanics can be studied and modelled at zero cost, no paywall issue
2. **A legal-to-recommend player onramp** — the module can *link* users to Catalyst's free PDF rather than reproduce it

That second point is a genuinely viable product pattern: **ship the engine, link the rules, ship no content.**

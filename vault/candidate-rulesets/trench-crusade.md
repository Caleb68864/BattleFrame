# Trench Crusade

Alternate-history WWI grimdark skirmish game. Free rules, aggressively closed license — the archetypal [[free-as-in-beer-vs-openly-licensed]] trap.

## Identity

- **Author/Publisher:** Tuomas Pirinen, Mike Franchina, Ivo Meulemans / Factory Fortress Inc.
- **URL:** https://www.trenchcrusade.com/rules/
- **Status:** Moved from "playtest" to official 1.0. Rules are a *living document*, updated on a schedule.

## Licensing — free to download, NOT openly licensed

**Confirmed.** The rules PDFs are free and the publisher states they always will be. This is **free-as-in-beer only**. There is no CC / OGL / ORC grant.

The [Community License](https://www.trenchcrusade.com/community-license/) is a **limited license that is personal, non-exclusive, non-sublicensable, non-transferable, and revocable**, permitting derivative works **for noncommercial community use only**. Terms found (via search-indexed excerpts of the license page — see verification caveat below) include:

- "You may not use the name 'Trench Crusade' or the names of any units, places, or copyrighted content in the titles or descriptions of your Projects."
- "You do not allow the use of the Trench Crusade IP, including any part of the rules and designs, for the creation of other board games, wargames, or other tabletop Projects."
- "Projects may not be commercialised or linked to a business entity in any way, shape, or form... includes, but is not limited to, crowdsourcing any part of its funding or content with a paywall (for example: Patreon, YouTube Premium, etc)."
- A broad grant-back: the creators "may use, copy, modify, distribute, and make derivative works of your Project on a royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable, transferable, sub-licensable, worldwide basis."
- Generative AI is prohibited anywhere in the creative process.

The separate ["Compatible with Trench Crusade" (TCC)](https://www.trenchcrusade.com/compatible-with-trench-crusade/) royalty-free license permits paid products — but **currently only accepts miniature projects that do not include rules**. Rules may be added to the program "in the future."

### Verdict for a Foundry module

**We could NOT ship Trench Crusade rules text or unit stat data.** A module reproducing stat blocks is exactly what the community license withholds, the TCC program currently excludes rules entirely, and the license is revocable at will. Designing *against* the game (a user supplies their own data) is the only defensible posture, and even that should get human legal review before shipping anything bearing the name.

> [!warning] Verification caveat
> trenchcrusade.com returns HTTP 403 to automated fetches, and web.archive.org was unavailable. The quoted license terms come from search-engine-indexed excerpts of the official license page, not from a direct read. The *direction* of the finding (closed, noncommercial, revocable, rules excluded from TCC) is consistent across multiple independent excerpts and is safe to rely on for a "cannot ship" decision. Any decision to ship anything requires a human reading the license page directly.

## Activation model

Not yet characterised — see [[trench-crusade]] follow-up needed. Reported to use an alternating-activation structure, but this was **not verified** against the rules PDF in this pass. Marked as a gap rather than guessed at.

## Suitability

**Poor as an early target, despite the free rules.** Licensing kills the project's core value proposition here — the whole reason to prefer free rules is to ship content, and this license specifically forbids that. Popularity makes it tempting; the license makes it a liability.

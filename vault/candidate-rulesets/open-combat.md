# Open Combat

Points-built pre-gunpowder skirmish. Despite the name, **"Open" refers to open-ended character building, not licensing** — it is a conventional all-rights-reserved commercial product.

## Identity

- **Author:** Carl Brown (former Games Workshop staffer), with development/mentoring by Gav Thorpe
- **Publisher:** Second Thunder — https://www.secondthunder.com/
- **Designer's page:** https://gavthorpe.co.uk/games/open-combat/

## Licensing — commercial, no open licence found

**Confirmed as to absence.** 102-page PDF and softback via Second Thunder / Amazon / DriveThruRPG / Wargame Vault. **No licence page, no OGL/CC grant, no third-party licence** found for Open Combat or for Second Thunder generally. Standard all-rights-reserved indie wargame. Not free.

> [!warning] The name is a false positive
> "Open Combat" surfaces in searches for open-licensed wargames purely on the word "open." It is not open content. Recorded here so the false lead isn't re-chased.

## Activation model — probably a stat-based activation roll, but genuinely unverified

**Unverified — the weakest-sourced item in this survey.**

What is confirmed: five stats — **Speed, Attack, Defence, Fortitude, Mind**. Attacks reduce **Fortitude** (HP-like) or **Mind** (morale-like). A figure may **forfeit its activation to regain 1 point of Fortitude or Mind**. Break point = average of total Fortitude and Mind.

What is not: club battle reports ([Chicago Skirmish Wargames](https://www.chicagoskirmishwargames.com/blog/2014/11/battle-report-open-combat-by-second/), [figurfanatikern](http://figurfanatikern.blogspot.com/2016/06/a-first-foray-into-open-combat.html)) describe activation as comparable to but distinct from [[song-of-blades-and-heroes]]' quality-roll system, noting that mounts/monsters "had trouble activating" — implying an activation roll with a failure chance tied to a stat. **No rulebook excerpt or authoritative source stating the actual activation formula was located.**

> [!danger] Do not repeat the activation model as fact
> Whether Open Combat uses alternating activation with a pass/fail roll, or something else entirely, is **not established**. The SBH resemblance is an inference drawn by bloggers from play experience. Consult the rulebook before citing this anywhere.

## Complexity

**Light-medium.** Designed around a "two sides of A4" core principle (per Gav Thorpe's account), 30–40 minute games, 24"x24" table, a handful of figures per side, **points-built characters with no stock profiles** (that's the "open" part), facing rules, recoil-on-hit.

> [!note] The no-stock-profiles design is interesting for a different reason
> A game with **no army lists** — every figure built from points at the table — inverts the usual module content problem. There *is* no roster data to ship or infringe; the shippable artifact would be a **character builder**. Games shaped like this are unusually well suited to a VTT and unusually cheap to make legally safe. Worth watching for the pattern even though this title is closed.

## Suitability

**Not a target.** Paid, closed, and its headline mechanic is unverified. The points-builder design pattern is the only takeaway.

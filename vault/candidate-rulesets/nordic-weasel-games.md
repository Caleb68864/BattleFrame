# Nordic Weasel Games — the "free titles" lead did not pan out

Investigated on the brief's suggestion that Ivan Sorensen's studio has free titles. **Could not confirm a single free, complete, standalone ruleset.**

## What was confirmed

Following the **June 2023 sale of Five Parsecs / Five Leagues / Weaseltech / the "Fivecore" series to Modiphius** (see [[five-parsecs-from-home]]), Ivan Sorensen **retains and independently publishes** — but **not for free** — titles including:

- Squad Hammer
- Rogue Hammer
- Clash on the Fringe
- Starport Scum
- Renegade Scout
- Shoot People in Space
- Knyghte Pyke Sworde

Source: [Nordic Weasel FAQ](https://nordicweasel.posthaven.com/faq-on-nordic-weasel-games-and-modiphius) (confirmed).

**"No Stars in Sight"** (hard sci-fi platoon game) is a **paid** title; no free edition found. *Partial* — [listing](https://www.wargamevault.com/product/141577/No-Stars-in-Sight-Hard-scifi-platoon-action) visible only via search index, not fetched directly.

## What could NOT be confirmed — and why that matters

**Unverified.** WargameVault and DriveThruRPG **blocked automated fetching** (HTTP 403 / CAPTCHA) on every attempt, so **no price tag was independently verified**.

Search-engine snippets (not directly fetched) reported two **$0.00** items:

- "Five Men in Normandy 30 cal edition"
- "Five Men at Kursk turn example"

> [!warning] These are almost certainly not free rulesets
> Both read as **example/reference documents** for a paid line — and *Five Men in Normandy* and *Five Men at Kursk* are themselves part of the **paid Fivecore line acquired by Modiphius** (confirmed above). A "turn example" is a demo document, not a ruleset. Treat "Nordic Weasel has free titles" as **unverified and probably false as stated**.

**No evidence was found that any Nordic Weasel or Modiphius title carries an open (CC/OGL/ORC) licence.** Everything located is conventional copyright, pay-to-download.

## The honest state of this finding

This note exists to record a **negative result properly** rather than let a plausible-sounding lead persist. The brief listed "Nordic Weasel Games' free titles" as a likely candidate; investigation found **no confirmed free standalone ruleset and no open licence**.

The storefronts block bots, so this could not be driven to certainty. **A human can settle it in about two minutes** by loading the Nordic Weasel storefront in a browser and sorting by price. Until someone does, the correct status is "not found," not "none exist."

## Suitability

**No candidate identified.** Nothing here to target unless the human check surfaces something the bot-blocking hid.

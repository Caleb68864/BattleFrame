# Zona Alfa

STALKER-flavoured exclusion-zone skirmish. Another Osprey title — commercial, closed, and not free.

## Identity

- **Author:** Patrick Todoroff
- **Publisher:** Osprey Games (Osprey Wargames series #25)
- **URL:** https://www.ospreypublishing.com/us/zona-alfa-9781472835697/

## Licensing — commercial, all rights reserved

**Confirmed as to absence of an open licence.** A conventional Osprey retail product (softback + PDF) via Osprey / Wargame Vault / DriveThruRPG at normal retail price. Targeted searching for an open licence found **none** — no OGL, ORC, CC, or bespoke third-party licence.

The [[osprey-games-blanket-copyright-policy]] applies. **Cannot ship content.**

> [!note] Not even free-as-in-beer
> Worth stating plainly: Zona Alfa isn't a free game at all, so it fails the survey's inclusion criterion on both axes. It appeared on the candidate list as a game worth *verifying*, and the verification came back negative. Recorded so it isn't re-investigated later.

## Activation model — alternating, model-by-model

**Partial.** Osprey's own [designer blog](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/us/osprey-blog/2020/designer-blog-zona-alfa-the-basics/) names the design pillars as "Streamlined Game Stats, **Alternating Activation**, and Troop Quality Levels." Uses **D10s**. Played on a 3'x3'/4'x4' area with a turn limit.

The alternating-activation claim is corroborated by Osprey's designer blog and retailer copy, but **the verbatim rulebook text describing the exact activation procedure was not obtained** — whether there's an activation roll, how many actions, and whether failure passes initiative are all **unverified**. Given how many neighbours in this survey ([[song-of-blades-and-heroes]], [[rampant-system]], [[this-is-not-a-test]]) hide a roll-to-continue mechanic inside the phrase "alternating activation," **do not assume this is the plain default.**

## Complexity

**Light-medium.** "Simple, fast-play skirmish rules," 1–2 hour games. *Partial* — secondary-source consensus, not verified against the rulebook.

## Suitability

**None.** Paid, closed, Osprey, and mechanically undistinguished on current evidence. Lowest priority in the survey — no licensing route and no unusual mechanic to justify study.

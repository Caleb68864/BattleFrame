# This Is Not a Test (TNT)

Post-apocalyptic skirmish. Paid rulebook + genuinely free demo/scenarios, all rights reserved. Markets its **"No I-Go-U-Go"** activation as a selling point.

## Identity

- **Publisher:** World's End Publishing — https://worldsendpublishing.com/
- **Author:** **Not found.** The site credits the company; no named individual designer located. *Unverified.*
- **Status (live check 2026-07-16):** the paid rulebook product page now redirects to the homepage — World's End is mid-transition to a **"TNT 2.0"** edition.

## Licensing — free demo, all rights reserved

**Confirmed** (verified live on the storefront, 2026-07-16).

- **Core rulebook:** paid commercial PDF (historically $19.99).
- **Genuinely free ($0.00) downloads:** "This is Not a Test Demo Rules," plus free scenarios — "Skull-Deep in the Dead," "The Jitters," and "Going It Alone" (solo rules).
- **No open licence.** No CC, no OGL, no third-party/compatibility licence.

This is the textbook [[free-as-in-beer-vs-openly-licensed|free-as-in-beer]] middle ground: real free content, zero redistribution rights. **We could not ship the demo rules either** — free to download is not free to redistribute.

> [!note] The 2.0 transition is a timing signal
> A publisher rebuilding its edition is *simultaneously* the worst time to build against it (rules churn) and a plausible moment to **ask about licensing**, since terms are being reconsidered anyway. Low-probability, low-cost ask.

## Activation model — dynamic, publisher-branded "no IGOUGO"

**Confirmed** as to framing — direct quote from the [official About page](https://worldsendpublishing.com/pages/about-this-is-not-a-test):

> "No I go U go. Letting one player activate his entire warband before you can go is outdated and unrealistic. Instead TNT uses a dynamic activation system where at any time play can switch between players."

**Partial** as to mechanics (from [1d6chan](https://1d6chan.miraheze.org/wiki/This_Is_Not_A_Test) and [blog reviews](http://bobbackwards71.blogspot.com/2015/05/this-is-not-test-rules-review.html), not the rulebook):

- Hybrid of IGOUGO and alternating activation.
- The player with initiative picks a model and rolls a skill test: **d10 + "Mettle" vs a target number**.
- **Success → 2 actions, and you may activate another model** (you keep going).
- **Failure → 1 action, and initiative-rolling passes to the opponent.**

> [!tip] Another member of the "greed ends your turn" family
> This is a third independent instance of the [[song-of-blades-and-heroes]] / [[rampant-system]] pattern: **keep activating until a roll fails, then control passes.** TNT softens it (a failure still yields 1 action rather than nothing).
>
> Three unrelated designers converging on this makes it a **first-class scheduler requirement**, not an exception to bolt on later. See [[activation-model-comparison]].

## Complexity

**Medium.** Per publisher marketing: 6 factions, 60+ mutations/skills, 60+ weapons, a campaign system, "hundreds of game hours" of playtesting. Crunchier than [[song-of-blades-and-heroes]]; lighter than Necromunda.

## Suitability

**Not an early target.** Licensing gives nothing, the edition is in flux, and the complexity is mid-weight. Useful as corroboration for the roll-to-continue activation family.

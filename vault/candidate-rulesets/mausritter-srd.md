# Mausritter SRD — CC BY 4.0

Not a wargame, but its **SRD is genuinely CC BY 4.0** and its licensing architecture is the model to copy. Included as a licensing exemplar, not an implementation target.

## Identity

- **Author:** Isaac Williams / Losing Games
- **URL:** https://mausritter.com/

## Two distinct instruments — don't conflate them

Mausritter runs **two separate licences**, and the difference is instructive.

### 1. The SRD License — true open content (CC BY 4.0)

**Confirmed**, fetched from https://mausritter.com/srd/terms/:

> "The text of the Mausritter System Reference Document (SRD) is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). This means you can use, copy, and modify the rules, mechanics, and text of the Mausritter SRD in your own works, as long as you follow the terms outlined below."

Required attribution:

> "This work is based on Mausritter, a product of Losing Games and Isaac Williams, and is licensed for use under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence."

Commercial use explicitly allowed. **Scope limit, stated by the publisher:** "The Mausritter book, illustrations and other materials that are not part of the SRD are not covered by this licence."

### 2. The Third Party Licence — bespoke compatibility, NOT CC

**Confirmed**, fetched from https://mausritter.com/third-party-licence/:

> "This licence allows anyone to make adventures, beasts, spells or hacks for Mausritter and sell or publish for free... without express permission from Losing Games."

- **May not:** copy/translate art or text of the Mausritter book (outside SRD terms); use Losing Games / Games Omnivorous / Mausritter logos; imply official status.
- **May:** use/modify item card templates and art; use/reference/modify game rules and mechanics; reference locations, creatures, characters, factions.
- Required text: "[Product name] is an independent production by [Author or Publisher] and is not affiliated with Losing Games. It is published under the Mausritter Third Party Licence." Plus "Mausritter is copyright Losing Games."

## Why it's in this vault

**It is not a skirmish wargame** — it's a rules-light OSR RPG, so it has no activation model and isn't a candidate. It earns a note for two reasons:

1. **It's the cleanest worked example of the pattern that actually unblocks this project:** carve the *mechanics* out into an SRD, CC-BY that, keep the book's prose and art reserved. This is exactly the shape of grant a Foundry module needs, and it's the concrete thing to point at when asking an indie designer like [[turnip28]]'s Max FitzGerald for terms — "would you do what Mausritter did?" is a much easier conversation than an abstract licensing request.
2. It confirms the **third licensing category** ([[mork-borg-third-party-license]], Mausritter TPL) that the free-vs-open binary misses.

## Skirmish-adjacent Mausritter content

**No official Mausritter skirmish wargame exists** (confirmed). The [Mausritter Library](https://library.mausritter.com/tag/skirmish/) lists fan supplements tagged "Skirmish" (e.g. by Matthew Morris, 2024) published under the Third Party Licence — unofficial fan content, not Losing Games products, individual rules not examined (**partial**). These would inherit the TPL, not the CC BY SRD grant, unless stated otherwise.

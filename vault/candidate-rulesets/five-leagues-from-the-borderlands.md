# Five Leagues from the Borderlands

Fantasy sibling of [[five-parsecs-from-home]]. Paid, Modiphius-owned. Its **skill-gated "quick phase"** is a distinctive activation idea.

## Identity

- **Author:** Ivan Sorensen
- **Publisher:** Modiphius (since the [[five-parsecs-from-home|June 2023 acquisition]])
- **URL:** https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/280863/Five-Leagues-from-the-Borderlands

## Licensing — paid, no open licence

**Confirmed.** Sold as a PDF (236 pages, ~£16) via DriveThruRPG / WargameVault under Modiphius. **No free or open-licence edition found.** Same corporate ownership as Five Parsecs — so the same closed permission route applies. **Cannot ship content.**

## Activation model — roll per model to act *before* the enemy

**Partial** (secondary review sources; not the rulebook):

- Solo skirmish.
- Each round, **roll a d6 per warband model against that character's Agility**. Successes let that character act in a **"quick phase" before the enemies**.
- Remaining figures roll initiative normally (Agility vs roll) to determine whether they act **before or after the enemy AI**.
- Ranged attacks use a two-die **"kill die" / "shock die"** system producing knockdown, out-of-action, pin, or flee.
- **All enemies share one AI behaviour table** — except "horrors of the night," which simply charge.

Sources: [albatwowarblog review](https://albatwowarblog.blogspot.com/2022/07/five-leagues-from-borderlands-review.html), [therewillbe.games discussion](https://therewillbe.games/forum/20-table-top-miniature-games/209438-five-leagues-from-the-borderlands).

> [!tip] Initiative as a per-model property, not a turn order
> The interesting inversion: there's no single initiative sequence. **Each model independently rolls to determine which side of the enemy activation it falls on.** The round is a *partition* — some of your models act before the enemy, the rest after — rather than a queue.
>
> An engine modelling turn order as an ordered list of participants can't express this cleanly. It's closer to "each model has a slot assignment rolled each round." Distinct from [[five-parsecs-from-home]]'s reaction-roll (*how many* act) — this one is about *when*. See [[activation-model-comparison]].

## Complexity

**Medium-heavy.** Full campaign game, like its sibling.

## Suitability

**No route** — paid and corporately owned. Retained for the per-model initiative partition, which is one of the more genuinely unusual structures found.

# Space Gits

Dexterity-driven drunk-orc skirmish game. Notable for a **real CC BY 4.0 grant** — but only over a component, not the game.

## Identity

- **Author:** Mike Hutchinson (also of [[gaslands-refuelled]])
- **Publisher:** Planet Smasher Games
- **URL:** https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/planetsmashergames/space-gits-miniatures-game

## Licensing — game is PAID; its dice engine is separately CC BY 4.0

**Confirmed, with a precise split:**

- **Space Gits itself** is a **commercial paid product**. No open licence. Same publisher as [[gaslands-refuelled]], whose [[fan-content-policies-are-not-open-licenses|fan content policy]] is restrictive. **Cannot ship its content.**
- **The underlying dice engine is separately released as an open SRD** — see [[riot-dice-srd]], confirmed **CC BY 4.0**, commercial use permitted.

Do not extrapolate the SRD's openness to the parent game. The grant is scoped to the SRD text, not to Space Gits' rules, units, or setting.

## Why it's worth a note anyway

1. **Existence proof.** A working commercial miniatures designer chose to CC-BY a mechanical subsystem while keeping the game paid. That's the "open engine, closed content" pattern — directly relevant to how this project might position itself. See [[riot-dice-srd]].
2. **Hutchinson is a plausible person to ask** about broader terms; he has demonstrably thought about open licensing.

## Activation model — dexterity, not scheduling

**Partial.** Movement and shooting both use a **physical dexterity mechanic**: players roll dice into the play area and models move directly toward where the dice land, producing a drunken stagger. Combat uses a rock-paper-scissors variant. The source **does not describe the turn structure or activation sequence** at all.

> [!note] Probably unimplementable, and that's the finding
> A game whose core resolution is *physically throwing dice onto a table and measuring where they landed* has no faithful VTT representation. This is a useful boundary marker: it identifies the edge of what a ruleset-neutral engine can host. Some games are out of scope for reasons that have nothing to do with licensing.

## Suitability

**Not a target.** Retained as a licensing precedent and as a scope boundary marker.

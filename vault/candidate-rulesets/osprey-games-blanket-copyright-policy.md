# Osprey Games' blanket copyright policy

One finding that disposes of an entire cluster of candidates at once.

## The policy

Osprey Publishing's [permissions page](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/us/connect/contact-us/permissions/) states:

> "All Osprey books are protected by copyright and so, unless otherwise stated, you are not permitted to copy or reproduce material from publications without our express permission."

Osprey Games is an imprint of Osprey Publishing, part of Bloomsbury Publishing Plc. The policy applies to Osprey Games titles by default. Product page footers carry "© Osprey Publishing 2026, Part of Bloomsbury Publishing Plc."

## What it rules out

Every Osprey Games title investigated in this survey is a standard all-rights-reserved commercial product with **no** CC/OGL/ORC grant:

- [[frostgrave]]
- [[rampant-system]] (Xenos Rampant, Lion Rampant 2e)
- [[gaslands-refuelled]]
- Zona Alfa — see [[zona-alfa]]

## The consistent pattern: free ancillaries, paid rules

Osprey's [gaming resources pages](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/uk/discover/gaming-resources/) offer genuinely free downloads for most titles — quick reference sheets, roster/army sheets, spell references, errata/FAQ, cards, templates. **None of these are the rules text.**

This is worth naming because it's a reliable false positive generator. Searching "free Frostgrave PDF" returns real, official, free Osprey PDFs — and none of them grant anything. The free ancillary is a marketing funnel for the paid rulebook, not an open-content gesture.

> [!note] Bloomsbury is a publisher, not an indie
> Osprey sits inside a large trade publisher. There is no realistic prospect of a bespoke permission grant for a hobbyist Foundry module, and the downside of guessing wrong is correspondingly larger than with a solo indie designer.

## Implication

Treat "published by Osprey Games" as a **near-conclusive disqualifier for content shipping**, checkable in seconds. Osprey titles remain useful as *mechanical* references — and they're mechanically excellent, which is why several appear in [[activation-model-comparison]] despite being unshippable.

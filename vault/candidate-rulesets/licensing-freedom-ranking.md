# Comparison: candidates ranked by licensing freedom

Ranking by **what we may legally ship**, the decisive axis. Every licence claim here is sourced in the linked note — follow the link before acting on any row.

## The ranking that actually matters: tractability of an ask

Licensing status is a snapshot; **permission is obtainable**. Ranked by likelihood of a yes:

1. **[[space-weirdos]]** — solo author, $5 itch.io game, licensing already raised in the comments. One email.
2. **[[forbidden-psalm]]** — author opted into an open ecosystem; asking for downstream terms is small.
3. **[[turnip28]]** — solo author, free-rules ethos, no licence *because none was written*, not because one was refused.
4. **[[song-of-blades-and-heroes]]** — Sfiligoi demonstrably licenses his engine to third parties.
5. ❌ **Osprey / Modiphius / GW** — corporate rights holders. Don't bother.

Point at [[mausritter-srd]] and [[riot-dice-srd]] when asking: *"would you publish a mechanics SRD under CC BY, like Mausritter did?"* is concrete, precedented, costs the designer nothing, and drives sales.

## The one-line summary

> **Almost nothing is shippable. Three texts are open, one is a wargame you could build. The real path is asking solo designers for mechanics-only SRD grants — and the precedents to cite already exist.**

## Notes on confidence

Ranked **partial**: individual licence findings are sourced and mostly confirmed, but the *ranking* and the tractability ordering are my judgement. Several sites (trenchcrusade.com, Patreon, WargameVault, GW) **block automated fetching**, so some claims rest on search-indexed excerpts rather than direct reads — flagged in each note. **No content should ship on the strength of these notes alone.** Every Tier 1/2 row needs a human to read the licence and confirm before use.

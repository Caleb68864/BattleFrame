# Fleet Construction: Points-Based, Fixed Ship Roster

Fleets are built with a **points budget**.

Verbatim: "**Each ship has a points rating you use to build your fleet.**"

Confirmed structure:
- Fleets are composed of **ships**, **drone groups**, and **mine packs** ([[drones-and-mines]]).
- Ships come from a **fixed catalogue** with pre-set stats and point costs, published on **ship cards**.
- A community **fleet builder** exists at https://bfv.cerkit.com/fleet-builder — "Build your fleet with ships, drone groups, and mine packs. Points auto-sum as you go." It filters by faction and includes an inventory checker (required vs. printed models).

**Critical constraint — no custom ship design:** "**No system for designing custom ships exists in the current rules.**" You pick from the published catalogue; you do not build a hull and bolt weapons to it. This substantially simplifies any engine's data model — ships are **static data records**, not composed entities.

**Not found:**
- Standard points levels for a game (e.g. is there a "500 pts" default?).
- Force-composition restrictions (min/max ships, mandatory flagship, faction purity requirements).
- Individual ship point costs.

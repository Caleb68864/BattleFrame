# Firing Arcs and Shield Arcs

Ships have **directional arcs** used for both attack and defence.

Confirmed:
- Weapons fire through **specific arcs** — "Ships target through specific arcs (like Front/Port)".
- Shields are **per-arc**, listed "in each colored ship shield arc on the left side of the ship card" (official FAQ) — see [[shield-dice-defense]].
- Arcs are **colour-coded** on the ship card.

**Not found:**
- The complete arc list. "Front/Port" is given as an example; **Front/Port/Starboard/Aft is the obvious four-arc assumption but is NOT confirmed by any source** — do not treat as fact.
- The angular width of each arc.
- Whether attack arcs and shield arcs use the same divisions (**likely, but unverified**).

**Engine note:** arcs make **facing** a first-class property of every ship, and mean incoming damage must be resolved against the *specific arc* it arrives through. Combined with the turn-rating constraint ([[tractor-beams]]) and free-form inches ([[measurement-in-inches]]), the spatial model needs true 2D position + orientation, not just position.

# Known Criticism: Initiative Is Fiddly, Movement Isn't Newtonian

Reviewers consistently praise the [[ship-card-is-the-state-container|card/energy system]] but criticise the **initiative and movement model**.

Verbatim from Bob's Miniature Wargaming Blog:

> "the initiative and more mechanics are **fiddly and confusing in practice** and don't seem to follow Newtonian physics (i.e., **don't power your engine and your ship stops?!? what?**)"

The physics objection targets [[drifting-and-voluntary-power-down]]: an unpowered ship in space should *coast*, but in Valkyrie an Engine-0 ship that didn't move last turn simply **stops dead**. Momentum is not conserved.

**Documented house rule:** one group ("Bruce") fixed this "by adding some **pre-plotting of moves** (retention of momentum, accelerate or decelerate, turns) which allows for **simultaneous action**." This replaces the [[movement-alternating-half-all-half|half/all/half]] schedule entirely with simultaneous written orders.

Separately, playtesters found the endgame chaotic due to [[ship-explosion-chain-reaction]].

**Why this matters for an engine:** the initiative/movement model is the part of this game most likely to be **replaced or house-ruled**. If building an engine, make [[initiative-via-sensor-roll|initiative]] and the movement schedule **pluggable strategies**, not hardcoded — there is already real-world demand for a momentum-based simultaneous alternative.

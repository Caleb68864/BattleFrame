# Rulebook: Short, Free, and Rapidly Versioned

**Length — confirmed short.** "The rulebook spans only **14 pages with 9 pages of actual rules**." Reviewers emphasise brevity and suitability for newer players. The publisher: "the rules are brief and simple. No complex subsystems here."

**Versioning — the rules move fast.** Evidence of at least four distinct versions:

| Version | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **v1.6** | Facebook post: "We've uploaded a revised rulebook for Battlefront Valkyrie, rules version 1.6" |
| **v4.5** | Official FAQ header: "Current Rules (Version 4.5)" |
| **v5.6** | Official FAQ: "Pending Updates (Rulebook v5.6)" — including [[tractor-beams|§9.4 Grabbed and Dragged]] |

**This is a live, actively-revised ruleset, not a stable target.** Jumping 1.6 → 4.5 → 5.6 within roughly a year means any engine built against it needs a **version-pinned rules layer**. Notes in this vault reflect **v4.5 with v5.6 pending changes noted where known**.

The FAQ itself is the publisher's **errata channel** and is the best free source of rules detail found.

**Rulebook is free** — see [[licensing-proprietary-no-srd]] for the price-vs-licence distinction.

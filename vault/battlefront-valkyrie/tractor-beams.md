# Tractor Beams: Grabbed and Dragged

Tractor beams let a ship **tow another object or vessel**. Rules live in **Section 9.4** of the rulebook, titled "Grabbed and Dragged."

From the official FAQ's *pending updates* for **rulebook v5.6**:

> "Towed objects move with the towing ship. When dragging larger vessels, **the larger ship's turn rating applies**."

Two important extractions:
1. **Towed objects move with the tower** — the towing ship's movement propagates to its cargo.
2. **"Turn rating" is a confirmed ship stat** — an angular constraint on how sharply a ship can turn. When towing, the *larger* ship's (worse) turn rating governs. This is the only direct confirmation found that turn rating exists as a stat.

A ship may **voluntarily power down specifically to be towed** ([[drifting-and-voluntary-power-down]]).

Tractor beams are also listed as one of the "other forces" that can move a stationary Engine-0 ship.

**Not found:** range, power cost, opposed roll (if any), whether an unwilling enemy ship can be tractored, and the numeric turn ratings.

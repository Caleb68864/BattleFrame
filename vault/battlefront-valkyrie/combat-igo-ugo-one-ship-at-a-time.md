# Combat Order: IGO-UGO, One Ship at a Time

The Combat Phase resolves with players **strictly alternating, firing one ship's weapons at a time**.

Verbatim: "Combat then ensues with each player firing one ship's weapons in an IGO-UGO fashion."

Key points:
- The alternation unit is **one ship's entire weapon complement**, not one weapon and not the whole fleet.
- Damage is applied immediately, so ships destroyed earlier in the phase do not fire later — **strongly implied but not explicitly confirmed**. This matters enormously given [[ship-explosion-chain-reaction]].

**Not found:**
- Who fires first in the Combat Phase — presumably the [[initiative-via-sensor-roll|initiative winner]], but **this is not stated in any source found and must not be assumed**.
- How alternation handles unequal fleet sizes (what happens when one player runs out of unfired ships).

**Note the asymmetry with movement:** combat is plain alternating (1:1), while movement is the half/all/half split ([[movement-alternating-half-all-half]]). These are *different ordering algorithms in the same round*.

# Repair Phase

The round ends with a **Repair Phase** in which "ships attempt to restore engine and hull points."

Confirmed:
- It is the **fourth and final phase** of the round ([[round-structure]]).
- It can restore **both** Hull and Engine ([[hit-location-hull-vs-engine]]).
- The word **"attempt"** implies a **dice roll**, not automatic recovery.

**Not found:**
- The repair roll mechanic (die type, target number).
- How much is restored per success.
- Whether repair costs [[reserve-power-allocation|reserve power cubes]] (**plausible — the power cubes go on "boxes/abilities," and repair is an obvious candidate box — but this is inference, explicitly unverified**).
- Whether a destroyed ship can be repaired (presumably not).
- Whether repair is capped at starting values.

**Engine note:** in-game repair means Hull/Engine are *recoverable* resources within a single battle, which is unusual and materially changes attrition modelling. This partially offsets [[engine-damage-death-spiral]].

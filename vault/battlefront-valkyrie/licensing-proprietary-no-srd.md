# Licensing: Free-to-Download, But Proprietary — No SRD Found

**Distinguish two separate things: price and licence.**

**Price — confirmed free.** The rulebook is available at **no cost**. The publisher's own product page states "Free rulebook download available through WargameVault." It is listed as both "BATTLEFRONT VALKYRIE: SAMPLE RULEBOOK" and "battlefront valkyrie: rulebook" (product 499502) on WargameVault and DriveThruRPG. **You do not need to buy the rules to play.** The paid products are the **models** (Starter Set FDG0400/496401, Expansion Set 1 FDG0433) — this is the classic "free rules, sell the minis" model, which for a 3D-printable game means selling STLs.

**Licence — no open licence found.**
- **No SRD found.**
- **No Creative Commons licence found.**
- **No OGL / ORC licence found.**
- The name is **trademarked** — the publisher consistently writes "Battlefront Valkyrie™" and "DRAGONLOCK™".

**Marked `partial`:** the *absence* of an open licence is inferred from its non-appearance across product pages, the publisher site, and the FAQ. The rulebook PDF's own copyright page could not be read (storefront returned HTTP 403) and is the authoritative source. **Default assumption: standard proprietary copyright, free distribution of the PDF only.**

**Implication for an engine project:** free-to-read rules do **not** grant rights to redistribute rules text, ship stats, or ship cards. A clean-room implementation of *mechanics* (rules aren't copyrightable; their expression is) is a materially different legal question from shipping the stat blocks. **Get legal review before shipping ship data.**

# Attack Roll Resolution: d6 Pool, Hit on 4+

Combat is a **d6 dice-pool system with a fixed target number**, not an opposed roll.

Procedure:
1. Attacker rolls a **number of d6 determined by the weapon** ("Weapons deal damage expressed in d6s").
2. Apply the **range modifier** ([[range-bands-and-dice-modifiers]]): short range **+1d6**, long range **−1d6**.
3. Each die showing **4, 5, or 6 is a hit**.
4. The **die face also determines hit location** — see [[hit-location-hull-vs-engine]].
5. Defender rolls shield dice to block — see [[shield-dice-defense]].

The critical design elegance: **one roll determines both whether you hit and what you hit**. There is no separate location roll.

Weapons fire through **specific arcs** (e.g. Front/Port) — see [[firing-arcs]].

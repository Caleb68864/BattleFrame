# Campaign Rules: NOT FOUND

**No campaign system was found in any public source.** No evidence was found of:

- Persistent fleets between games
- Ship experience, veterancy, or upgrades
- Between-battle repair, salvage, or resupply
- Campaign maps or territory control
- Fleet rosters that carry damage forward

**This is a "not found," not a "does not exist."** Sources searched: publisher product pages, publisher FAQ, BGG (blocked 403), RPG PUB discussion thread, two review blogs, WargameVault/DriveThruRPG listings.

**Weak negative evidence** (why absence is plausible):
- The rulebook is only **14 pages, 9 of them rules** ([[rulebook-availability-and-versions]]) — very little room for a campaign system.
- The game has **no scenarios at all** beyond annihilation ([[victory-conditions-annihilation-only]]), and campaign systems normally sit on top of a scenario library.
- Reviewers describing the game's scope never mention campaign play.

Given the above, **absence of campaign rules is likely** — but it is **explicitly unverified inference and must not be relied upon**. The definitive check is the free rulebook PDF ([[rulebook-availability-and-versions]]), which could not be downloaded during this research (storefront returned HTTP 403).

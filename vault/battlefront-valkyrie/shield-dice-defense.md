# Shield Dice: Roll Equal to Arc Rating, Block on 5+

Defence is a **save roll**, resolved after the attacker's hits are known.

Procedure:
- Defender rolls **d6 equal to their shield rating** for the arc being hit.
- Each **5 or 6 blocks one hit**.
- **Shield rolls block hull damage first**, then engine damage. (Source: Miniature Mayhem — "Shield rolls prioritize blocking hull damage first before engine damage applies.") **Note: this is counterintuitive — it means engine hits are the ones that leak through.** Marked `partial`; single-source.

**Shields are per-arc and engine-dependent.** From the official FAQ (v4.5):

> "The number of shield dice a ship has is listed in each colored ship shield arc on the left side of the ship card, represented by two numbers separated by a slash (e.g., 3/2, 2/1, etc.)"

The **slash notation is the key structural detail**: two values per arc. Public sources confirm shields degrade with engine damage ("Shields are also based on the strength of the engines, as once your ship begins to take damage, the shields become less effective"), so the two numbers are almost certainly **full-power / reduced-power** shield dice. **This interpretation is `unverified` inference** — the FAQ states the notation but not what each number means.

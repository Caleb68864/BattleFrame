# Factions: Terran, Cygnian, Kurgun

The community fleet builder exposes a faction filter with **three factions**:

- **Terran**
- **Cygnian**
- **Kurgun**

Plus a separate catalogue entry for the **Asteroid Eater** (see [[asteroids]]), which appears to sit outside the three factions — possibly a neutral/monster unit.

**Marked `partial`:** this comes from the fleet builder UI (a community tool at bfv.cerkit.com), not from the publisher's own materials. The faction list is likely accurate but is **not** confirmed against the rulebook, and it may not be exhaustive — **Expansion Set 1 exists** (BGG boardgame/436470, FDG0433) and may add factions or ships. Expansion contents could not be retrieved.

**Not found:**
- Faction-specific special rules or asymmetries. **It is unknown whether factions differ mechanically at all, or are purely a cosmetic/ship-roster distinction.** Do not assume asymmetric faction rules exist.
- Ship classes within each faction.

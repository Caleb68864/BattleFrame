# Measurement: Free-Form Inches, No Grid or Zones

Battlefront Valkyrie uses **free-form tabletop measurement in inches**. It is **not** grid-based and **not** zone-based.

Evidence:
- "a multi-inch explosion template" ([[ship-explosion-chain-reaction]]) — direct reference to inches.
- The starter set ships with **templates** and physical **counters**, which is the signature of free-measurement miniatures play rather than a grid.
- Ships have a **turn rating** (see [[tractor-beams]]) — an angular constraint that only makes sense in free space.
- Reviewers play at **1/7000 scale** with physical asteroid terrain ([[asteroids]]).

**Marked `partial`:** no source found explicitly states the measurement unit in a rules-quotation sense, nor exact movement distances per ship class. The inference from "multi-inch template" plus templates-and-miniatures format is strong but is inference.

**Not found:**
- Movement rates per ship class.
- Range band distances in inches ([[range-bands-and-dice-modifiers]]).
- Whether the game specifies a standard table size.
- Whether the map has hard edges — **it does**: drifting off the map destroys a ship ([[drifting-and-voluntary-power-down]]), so a bounded play area is confirmed even if its dimensions are not.

# Asteroids: Terrain With Size Classes

Asteroids are physical 3D-printed terrain included in the starter set, described by the publisher as being "for strategic gameplay and scenic enhancement."

Confirmed from the official FAQ:
- Asteroids have **size classes** — the FAQ explicitly references "**a medium/large asteroid**", so at least **small / medium / large** exist.
- A **drifting ship that hits a medium or large asteroid is destroyed** ([[drifting-and-voluntary-power-down]]). The size distinction is mechanically load-bearing: small asteroids evidently do *not* destroy drifting ships.
- Asteroids are one of the "other forces" that can act on a stationary Engine-0 ship.

There is also an "**Asteroid Eater**" ship model listed in the [[fleet-construction-points|fleet builder]] catalogue, alongside the three factions — suggesting asteroids interact with at least one special unit.

**Not found:**
- What happens when a **powered** (non-drifting) ship contacts an asteroid.
- Whether asteroids block line of sight or weapons fire.
- Whether asteroids move or drift themselves.
- Asteroid placement/deployment rules.

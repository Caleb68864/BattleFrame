# Hit Location: Die Face Selects Hull or Engine

The **value on the attack die determines the damage type**:

| Die face | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Miss |
| **4–5** | **Hull damage** |
| **6** | **Engine damage** |

Verbatim: "The value on the dice rolled to hit also determine the hit type Hull 4-5 or Engine 6." And: "Rolling 4-5 damages the hull; rolling a 6 damages the engine specifically."

This means **1/6 of all dice rolled threaten the engine** and 2/6 threaten the hull — engine damage is intrinsically rarer but far more punishing ([[engine-damage-death-spiral]]).

There are only **two damage tracks**: Hull and Engine. Both are tracked with **dial counters** included in the starter set. There is no separate wound/crew/system track.

**No status-condition system was found** — no suppression, morale, pinning, or similar. The only "state" a ship carries is Hull value, Engine value, and powered-down/drifting status ([[drifting-and-voluntary-power-down]]).

# Game Identity: Battlefront Valkyrie

**Battlefront Valkyrie™** is a 3D-printable **spaceship fleet combat wargame** published by **Fat Dragon Games** (the DRAGONLOCK terrain company, in business since 2005).

Publisher framing: "a groundbreaking 3D printable wargame that delivers the thrill of epic fleet battles right to your tabletop, all at a cost that's just a fraction of traditional games."

Key identifying facts:
- Product code **FDG0400** (Starter Set: FDG0496401 on WargameVault).
- All models are optimized for FDM printing and **print without supports**.
- Released 2024–2025; BGG entry exists at boardgame/431615.
- Reviewers place it "on the lighter side of miniature wargaming."

**This is a fleet/ship game, not an infantry skirmish game.** Models are ships, not individual soldiers. See [[rejected-candidates]] for why this is nonetheless the correct identification.

# Suppression Tokens — Status Cleared by Spending Control Points

Suppression exists and is tracked with **tokens** on units.

**Confirmed:** a player may spend a **control point** to **remove a unit's suppression token** — one of the three known control point sinks ([[control-point-economy]]). This is the only fully sourced fact about suppression.

Two implications follow from that sourced fact alone:
- Suppression **persists** until actively cleared (it is not automatic end-of-round cleanup), and
- clearing it **competes directly** with buying priority orders and asset cards. Suppression's real cost is the tempo you spend recovering from it.

The reviews list "suppression, morale, and leaning out" as the rules that add tactical depth beyond the core loop.

## Unknowns (not found — do not guess these)

- **What causes suppression.** Being shot at? Being missed? A specific weapon trait? Failing a morale check? Not stated in any source found.
- **What suppression does to a unit** — reduced actions? attack penalty? cannot react? Not stated anywhere public.
- Whether suppression **stacks** (multiple tokens on one unit). The phrasing "a unit's suppression token" (singular) hints at a binary flag, but that is **inference, not confirmed**.
- Interaction with [[morale]] and with [[reaction-token-overwatch]].

This is a significant gap: the task brief correctly anticipated suppression would matter in a modern-warfare game, and INX clearly makes it a first-class subsystem — but its actual effect is not documented in public sources. **The free digital rulebook would be required to close this.**

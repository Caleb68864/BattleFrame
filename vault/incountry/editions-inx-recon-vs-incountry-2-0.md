# Editions — INX:RECON vs INCOUNTRY 2.0

At least two distinct rules products exist under the INX brand. **They are not the same ruleset**, and sources about one do not automatically describe the other.

- **INX:RECON** — the earlier, cut-down two-player introductory game. Sold as its own rulebook (https://gcmini.mybigcommerce.com/inxrecon-rule-book/). Supporting PDFs: "INCOUNTRY: RECON Game Overview" (https://www.scribd.com/document/662772311/INXRECON-Digital) and "INCOUNTRY: RECON Forces & Scenarios" (https://www.scribd.com/document/662772313/INX-RECON-FORCES-SCENARIOS). Described as "the simple, fast paced, two player game."
- **INX 2.0 (Modern Warfare)** — the current full ruleset. Print rules $15 USD (https://inxcountry.com/products/inx-rules); a free digital version exists; a Premium Rules Bundle and a separate **INX 2.0: Armored Combat** PDF (https://inxcountry.com/products/incountry-2-0-digital-rules-copy) are also sold.

**Confidence caveat:** most mechanical notes in this vault derive from reviews of the **2.0 starter box**. Attribution of any given mechanic to RECON specifically is unverified. The tier-based force construction described in [[force-construction-tiers-and-teams]] comes from a **RECON** document and may not match 2.0.

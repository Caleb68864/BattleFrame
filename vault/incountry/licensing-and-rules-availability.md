# Licensing — Free Digital Rules, No SRD

INX 2.0 rules are **available free in digital form**, but there is **no SRD and no open licence**.

- **INCOUNTRY 2.0: Digital Rules — $0.00 USD** (https://inxcountry.com/products/incountry-digital-rules). Free, but **not a direct download**: "This digital file will be sent to your email upon check out." Requires a Shopify checkout with an email address. The listing showed "Inventory on the way," so availability may be intermittent.
- **INX 2.0 Rules (print) — $15.00 USD** (https://inxcountry.com/products/inx-rules)
- **INX 2.0 Premium Rules Bundle** — paid (https://inxcountry.com/products/inx-2-0-premium-rules-pack)
- **INX 2.0: Armored Combat PDF** — paid, separate (https://inxcountry.com/products/incountry-2-0-digital-rules-copy)
- **2 Player Starter Box — $150.00 USD** (https://inxcountry.com/products/2-player-starter-box)
- EU distributor Desk-Ops also lists the free 2.0 digital rules (https://www.desk-ops.de/en/Miniatures/Brands/Echo-Dark-Studios/)

**No SRD, no Creative Commons licence, no community-use policy was found.** Free-to-read is not free-to-implement: nothing found grants rights to reproduce the rules in a digital engine. **Any VTT implementation should be treated as needing Echo Dark Studios' permission** — contact via https://inxcountry.com/pages/contact or their Discord (https://discord.gg/qfHWWUxJHJ).

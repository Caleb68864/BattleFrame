# Movement and Measurement — Speed Value, Units Not Confirmed

**Confirmed:** a unit "moves up to its **speed value**" on activation ([[action-economy-move-then-shoot-or-react]]). Speed is therefore a per-model stat carried on the unit's card ([[force-construction-tiers-and-teams]]).

**Confirmed:** the game is played on a **2'x2' map, expandable to 3'x3'** ([[table-size-and-game-length]]), and is a 28mm miniatures game — which makes **inches** the near-certain measurement unit, but **no source found states the unit or any actual speed value.** Recorded as inference, not fact.

## Unknowns (not found)

- Measurement unit (inches assumed, **unverified**) and actual speed values.
- Whether measurement is **free/pre-measurable** or estimated — relevant to hidden information ([[hidden-information-and-fog-of-war]]).
- Terrain effects on movement: difficult terrain, climbing, vaulting. **Not found.**
- Whether there is a run/sprint option trading the shoot action for extra movement. Given the strict move-then-shoot-or-react structure ([[action-economy-move-then-shoot-or-react]]), there is **no evidence** of one — but absence of evidence here is thin, since reviews summarise rather than enumerate.
- Base-to-base / engagement rules. **Not found.**

Movement is one of the least-documented areas in public sources, likely because reviewers treat it as unremarkable.

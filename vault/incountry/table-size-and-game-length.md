# Table Size and Game Length — 2'x2', 30–45 Minutes, Contact by Round Two

Physical and temporal scale, confirmed by two independent reviews:

- **Table:** 2'x2', expandable to 3'x3'.
- **Length:** 30–45 minutes typical (also stated by the publisher, https://inxcountry.com/pages/game).
- **Scale:** 28mm miniatures.
- **Force size:** 3 units per side ([[force-construction-tiers-and-teams]]).
- **Tempo:** "combat occurring **from round two onward** due to compact starting positions" (https://www.boardgamequest.com/inx-incountry-review/).

## Why this matters architecturally

The tiny board plus early contact is the design constraint that explains the rest of the ruleset: with everything in range almost immediately, tension has to come from **reactions** ([[reaction-token-overwatch]]) and **lethality** ([[damage-armor-saves-and-headshots]]) rather than from manoeuvre or approach.

It also underpins the inference in [[hidden-information-and-fog-of-war]] that INX is likely an open-state game — there is little board to hide on.

For a VTT engine: small board, ~6 total models, binary model state. The **state space is tiny**; the complexity is entirely in the **trigger/interrupt system**, not in scale.

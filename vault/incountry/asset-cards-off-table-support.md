# Asset Cards — Purchasable Off-Roster Effects

**Asset cards** are a card-based effect system bought with control points.

**Confirmed:** one of the three control point sinks is to **"bring an asset card into play"** ([[control-point-economy]]). Confirmed by two independent reviews.

That is the extent of confirmed knowledge. Asset cards are named but never described in any public source found.

## Unknowns (not found — this is the biggest content gap)

- **What assets actually are.** The task brief asks specifically about off-table support (artillery, air strikes). Asset cards are the **structurally likely home** for such effects — a player-level resource spent to inject an effect not tied to any model on the roster. But **no source found states what a single asset card does.** This is flagged as the natural place to look, **not** as a claim that artillery/air support exists. **Do not record off-table fire support as an INX mechanic without the rulebook.**
- Whether assets are drawn from a deck, chosen from a hand, or selected during force construction.
- Whether assets are one-shot or persistent.
- Whether asset cards are hidden from the opponent before deployment — relevant to [[hidden-information-and-fog-of-war]].
- Cost in control points.

## Why this matters architecturally

Even at confirmed level, asset cards establish that **not all game effects originate from a model on the table.** A player-scoped resource can spawn effects into play. An engine whose entity model assumes "everything that acts is a miniature with a position" needs an escape hatch for this.

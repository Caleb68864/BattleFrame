# Game Identity — INCOUNTRY (INX) by Echo Dark Studios

**INCOUNTRY** (styled `INCOUNTRY`, abbreviated **INX**) is a 28mm miniatures skirmish wargame published by **Echo Dark Studios LLC**.

- Official site: https://inxcountry.com/ (Shopify storefront)
- BoardGameGeek entry: https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/373595/incountry
- BGG rulebook entry: https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/375986/inx-incountry-rulebook
- EU distributor: https://www.desk-ops.de/en/Miniatures/Brands/Echo-Dark-Studios/
- AU retailer: https://ozwargaming.com.au/ and https://greenwoodgames.com.au/collections/incountry-modern-wargame

Marketed as "fast, ultramodern combat on your tabletop." The publisher's pitch is that the design team includes veterans and serving/retired military advisors, claiming it is "the sole wargame crafted by individuals with firsthand military experience."

**Designer/author:** not found. No individual designer is credited on the public product pages.

This is the game researched for this note set. See [[name-disambiguation-not-a-vietnam-game]] for why the "Vietnam" premise is wrong.

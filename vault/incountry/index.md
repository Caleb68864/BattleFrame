# INCOUNTRY (INX) — Research Index

Research notes on **INCOUNTRY (INX)** by **Echo Dark Studios LLC** — a 28mm modern skirmish wargame. https://inxcountry.com/

> [!warning] The Vietnam premise was wrong
> This research was commissioned on the assumption that "InCountry" is a Vietnam War game (from the soldier slang). **It is not** — INX is contemporary/ultramodern. A *separate* Vietnam game called "In Country" exists and was not researched. See [[name-disambiguation-not-a-vietnam-game]].

> [!caution] Public sources are thin
> Two hobby reviews plus publisher marketing carry nearly all confirmed detail. BGG returned 403; the Scribd PDFs exposed only metadata. **Several core mechanics — including what suppression and morale actually do, and how reactions trigger — are undocumented publicly.** Start at [[open-questions-and-gaps]] before designing against these notes.

## Turn structure — the core loop
- [[command-card-initiative]] — Each round you pick a card that sets both your initiative and your resource; **low initiative goes first**.
- [[activation-order-priority-units]] — Alternating activation in two passes: purchased priority units act before everyone else.
- [[control-point-economy]] — One player-level per-round budget feeding three unrelated subsystems.
- [[action-economy-move-then-shoot-or-react]] — Move up to speed, then shoot **or** bank a reaction. Two slots, no action points.

## Combat and state
- [[reaction-token-overwatch]] — The headline mechanic: banked overwatch/counter-attacks that break single-actor turn flow.
- [[combat-resolution-d10]] — Roll-under d10 pool against the weapon's attack value; hits aggregate into one damage number.
- [[damage-armor-saves-and-headshots]] — No hit points: exceed defence and the model dies outright, otherwise roll a roll-over save.
- [[suppression-tokens]] — Confirmed to exist and cost control points to clear; **what it does is unknown**.
- [[morale]] — Token-tracked, named in every review, **effect entirely undocumented**.
- [[lean-token-and-line-of-sight]] — Shoot from cover without exposure; makes LOS a function of declared state, not just geometry.

## Board, forces, and scenarios
- [[movement-and-measurement]] — Per-model speed value; the actual units and numbers were never published.
- [[table-size-and-game-length]] — 2'x2', 3 units a side, 30–45 minutes, contact by round two — the constraint explaining the whole design.
- [[objectives-and-missions]] — Five scenarios including board-escape and an asymmetric attack/defence; win conditions are pluggable.
- [[force-construction-tiers-and-teams]] — No points system at all: pick a force, pick three units.
- [[campaign-rules]] — Campaigns are referenced but **no persistence mechanic is documented anywhere**.

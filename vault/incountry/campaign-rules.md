# Campaign Rules — Referenced but Undocumented

Campaign play exists in the INX line, but **nothing about persistence between games is publicly documented.**

**Confirmed:** the publisher's game page references **"campaign expansion options including vehicles and bomb defusals"** (https://inxcountry.com/pages/game). Vehicles are a real product line (https://inxcountry.com/collections/vehicles) and **INX 2.0: Armored Combat** is a separate paid PDF (https://inxcountry.com/products/incountry-2-0-digital-rules-copy).

**Partial:** the **INCOUNTRY: RECON Forces & Scenarios** guide is described as including **campaign rules** (https://www.scribd.com/document/662772313/INX-RECON-FORCES-SCENARIOS). Only the document's metadata was retrievable; the body was not. Attribution to RECON rather than 2.0 — see [[editions-inx-recon-vs-incountry-2-0]].

## Unknowns (not found)

- **What persists between games** — this is the actual question the brief asked, and **no source answers it.** No evidence found for veterancy/XP, injury/casualty carry-over, requisition, territory, or a campaign map.
- Whether "campaign" here means a **linked narrative scenario sequence** (common in small skirmish games, and consistent with "bomb defusals" reading as a scenario type rather than a persistence rule) or a **true persistent-state campaign**. The phrasing "campaign expansion options including vehicles and bomb defusals" lists *content*, not *persistence* — which weakly suggests linked scenarios. **This is inference; do not record it as fact.**

For engine purposes: **assume nothing persists** until the rulebook is read. There is currently no sourced basis for a persistence layer.

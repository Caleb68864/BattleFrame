# The "Compatible with OPR" Programme Exists — But It's Aimed at Miniature Makers, Not Software

OPR runs an official partner programme, linked from the main site nav as **"OPR compatibility"**. It is the **only formal permission channel OPR is known to operate** — which makes it interesting, even though it does not fit a VTT module.

## What it is

Per the OPR Community Wiki: the programme lets creators *"design and publish models and rules compatible with the games made by OPR Games."* Carrying the **"Compatible with OPR"** banner means a creator either has OPR army lists that fit their models, or makes models that fit existing OPR army lists. Creators **apply through OPR's website**.

Announced partners are consistently **3D miniature companies** — EverOn Games (Jan 2026), JohnBearRoss (Aug 2024), Crippled God Foundry, Clay Beast Creation.

## Why it doesn't directly fit

The programme's shape is *"sell miniatures, we'll bless army lists for them"* — it flows in the opposite direction from a VTT module. Every announced partner is a physical/STL miniature maker. **No software or VTT partner was found.**

## Why it still matters

Three reasons not to dismiss it:

1. **It proves OPR grants permissions.** A studio with an application process and a partner banner is a studio that says yes to some people. That is the door referenced in [[licence-ask-opr-directly-is-the-only-clean-path]].
2. **It's an existing intake route.** Rather than a cold email, an approach can go through a channel OPR already staffs.
3. **It signals posture.** OPR actively *wants* an ecosystem around its free rules. Combined with [[api-the-tts-endpoint-is-precedent-for-vtt-import]], the picture is a studio that benefits from and tolerates third-party tooling.

**Do not mistake this for a licence you already hold.** The programme's terms were not located — **not found**: no public terms document, no statement of what rights the banner conveys, no fee structure. Being "OPR Compatible" may convey nothing beyond marketing use of a banner. It certainly does not on its own authorise [[licence-cannot-bundle-opr-rules-text-in-a-foundry-module]].

**Confidence: partial** — **confirmed** that the programme exists, is applied to via the website, and that all identified partners are miniature makers. **Unverified:** its actual terms, and whether software has ever been admitted.

# There Are Exactly Eight Current OPR Rulesets, Versioned in Three Families

Direct answer to "how many distinct rulesets are in the family?" — **eight current**, per OPR's own `/games` and `/resources` pages. No more.

| System | Official descriptor | Version |
|---|---|---|
| **Grimdark Future** (GF) | "Sci-Fi Battle Game" | v3.5.1 |
| **GF: Firefight** (GFF) | "Sci-fi Skirmish Game" | v3.5.1 |
| **GF: Star Quest** | "Sci-Fi Adventure Game" | v2.0.1 |
| **GF: Warfleets** | "Space Battle Game" | v2.2.0 |
| **Age of Fantasy** (AoF) | "Fantasy Battle Game" | v3.5.1 |
| **AoF: Skirmish** | "Fantasy Skirmish Game" | v3.5.1 |
| **AoF: Regiments** | "Regimental Battle Game" | v3.5.1 |
| **AoF: Quest** | "Fantasy Adventure Game" | v2.0.1 |

**Versioned in three separate families** — a real bookkeeping trap: the **five battle/skirmish games sit at 3.5.x**, the **two Quest games at 2.0.x**, and **Warfleets alone at 2.2.0**. They do **not** move together. Any version-pinning logic must be per-ruleset ([[mechanics-rules-versions-v2-vs-v3-differ-materially]]).

## The structure is a 4×2 grid

The line-up is a deliberate matrix of **scale × setting**:

| Scale | Sci-fi | Fantasy |
|---|---|---|
| Mass battle | Grimdark Future | Age of Fantasy |
| Skirmish | GF: Firefight | AoF: Skirmish |
| Adventure/dungeon | GF: Star Quest | AoF: Quest |
| Other | GF: Warfleets (fleet) | AoF: Regiments (rank-and-flank) |

Each sci-fi/fantasy pair is a **reskin of one ruleset** ([[family-gf-and-aof-are-the-same-engine-reskinned]]). The grid is only broken at the bottom row: Warfleets and Regiments have no counterpart.

**Effectively there are ~4-5 distinct rules engines, not 8** — which is the core of [[assessment-one-engine-can-host-most-of-the-family]].

## Naming correction

The current fleet game is **"Grimdark Future: Warfleets"**, *not* "Warfleets: FTL". The wiki states verbatim: *"Warfleets FTL has been superseded by Grimdark Future: Warfleets."* FTL was standalone with its own 6 factions; GF: Warfleets folds fleet combat into the GF setting.
